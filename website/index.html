How it works

Three steps. No willpower settings to fiddle with.

1

Choose what to block

Pick the apps that pull you in — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Niyyah watches for them, nothing else.

2

Open the app, read first

A verse or hadith appears instantly. The confirm button unlocks after a 10-second read — no skipping.

3

Confirm, then carry on

The app opens for the session you chose — 5 to 30 minutes. Leave early and the cycle starts fresh next time.
