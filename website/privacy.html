2. Newsletter signup (MailerLite)

When you register your email address in the signup form, we use it to send you early access information and release updates for Niyyah.

  • What is collected: your email address. For proof of consent, the IP address and timestamp of both the signup and the confirmation click are recorded.
  • Double opt-in: you receive a confirmation email and are only subscribed after you click its confirmation link. Without confirmation, no newsletters are sent and the address is deleted after a short period.
  • Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time — every email contains an unsubscribe link, or write to us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of past processing.
  • Processor: emails are sent and the list is managed by MailerLite (MailerLite, UAB, Vilnius, Lithuania), acting as our processor under a data-processing agreement. See MailerLite's privacy policy for details of their processing.
  • Retention: your address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. Confirmation records are kept to prove consent, as required.

4. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your address and the content of your message solely to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR). Once your request is resolved and no statutory retention duties apply, the correspondence is deleted.

5. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking
